## Full description

A hand-inked roguelike descent through five chapters of basement.

Choose one of four children and follow a red thread under the floorboards.
Clear rooms with tears and bombs, gather from a pool of 108 collectibles,
break curses, uncover secret rooms, and put down the keeper of each floor.
Every descent is seeded; a daily house is shared by everyone.

**Features**
- Five chapters: The Cellar, The Ossuary, The Drowned Gallery, The Bone
  Orchard and The Hollow.
- Four playable children, each with a different body, stat line, starting
  loadout and a cooldown skill.
- Five main bosses and six challenge keepers, 30 enemy types and six elite
  variants.
- 10 room types including secret, sacrifice, challenge, library and vault.
- 28 room furniture layouts, 8 interior dressings and 8 encounter rules, so
  rooms keep changing shape between descents.
- 108 items, 4 character skills, 6 transformations, 3 curses and 30 Ledger marks.
- A fully voiced opening, chapter, boss and miniboss narration. Death is silent.
- Floating touch D-pad, weep stick, bomb, active-relic and skill buttons;
  portrait and landscape both supported.
- Runs last roughly 30–50 minutes. No account, no ads, no purchases.

The house remembers every descent. Keep the thread.

## Data safety / privacy

- No account or sign-in.
- No third-party analytics or advertising SDK is included in the Android build.
- Progress (Ledger marks, settings, selected character, daily state) is stored
  locally on the device.
- No personal data is collected or shared.
- The game needs no network connection to play.

A hosted privacy-policy URL must be supplied in Play Console. The policy text
is in `privacy-policy.md`; host it and add a support contact.
