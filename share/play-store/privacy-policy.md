# BOUND BELOW — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

BOUND BELOW is a single-player offline game. It does not require an account and
does not collect, transmit or share personal information.

## Data stored on the device

The game stores progress locally on the device: Ledger marks and discoveries,
settings, the selected character, the daily seed state and the best-run record.
This data stays on the device and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

## Network, ads and purchases

The Android build does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs,
does not show ads and does not offer in-app purchases. The game is playable
without a network connection.

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the developer at the support email
listed on the Google Play store page.
